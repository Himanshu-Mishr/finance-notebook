> [!definition] FII (Foreign Institutional Investor)
> Foreign institutional investors (FIIs), also called foreign portfolio investors (FPIs), are overseas funds, pension funds and other large investors that buy Indian shares and bonds.

## Explain

FIIs bring large sums. When they buy in bulk, prices tend to rise, and when they sell in bulk, prices tend to fall. Their holding is therefore watched closely.

They respond to global conditions: US interest rates, the rupee, and risk appetite, as much as to Indian companies.

## Example

> [!example] Five points gone
> From 22.60% to 17.19% in about three years, a fall of 5.4 points. On today's market cap of ₹15.8 lakh crore, one percentage point is about ₹15,800 crore, so 5.4 points is on the order of ₹85,000 crore of ownership.
>
> Over the same period DIIs raised their holding (see [[dii|DII (Domestic Institutional Investor)]]).

> [!note] Data caveat
> Screener notes that classification of foreign investors changed from Sep 2022, so older figures may not be strictly comparable.
