## Market data and ratios
- [[fifty-two-week-high-low|52-Week High and Low]]: The highest and lowest prices a share traded at in the last year.
- [[book-value|Book Value]]: What the company's owners would own on paper after paying all debts.
- [[dividend-payout|Dividend Payout Ratio]]: The share of profit a company pays out as dividends.
- [[dividend-yield|Dividend Yield]]: The cash dividend you receive each year as a percentage of the share price.
- [[eps|Earnings Per Share]]: The profit that belongs to one share.
- [[earnings-yield|Earnings Yield]]: Yearly profit as a percentage of the share price: the flip side of P/E.
- [[face-value|Face Value]]: The nominal value printed on a share, used to count shares and record capital.
- [[market-capitalisation|Market Capitalisation]]: The total value the stock market puts on all of a company's shares.
- [[pe-ratio|P/E Ratio]]: How many rupees investors pay for each rupee of a company's yearly profit.
- [[price-to-book|Price to Book Ratio]]: How many times its book value the market pays for a company.
- [[stock-index|Stock Index]]: A basket of shares used to measure the market, like Nifty 50 and Sensex.

## Profit and loss
- [[depreciation|Depreciation]]: The yearly cost of the wear and ageing of machines, buildings and other long-life assets.
- [[interest-expense-and-coverage|Interest Coverage]]: How comfortably a company's operating profit covers its interest bill.
- [[net-profit|Net Profit]]: What is left after every cost, interest and tax has been paid.
- [[operating-profit|Operating Profit]]: Profit from running the core business, before interest, depreciation and tax.
- [[operating-profit-margin|Operating Profit Margin]]: The share of each rupee of sales left as operating profit.
- [[other-income|Other Income]]: Money a company earns from things other than its main business.
- [[profit-and-loss|Profit and Loss Statement]]: The report that shows how a company turns sales into profit over a period.
- [[profit-before-tax|Profit Before Tax]]: Profit after all costs except income tax, and the tax rate applied to it.
- [[sales-revenue|Sales (Revenue)]]: The total money a company earns from selling its goods and services.

## Balance sheet
- [[balance-sheet|Balance Sheet]]: A snapshot of what a company owns and owes on one date.
- [[borrowings|Borrowings]]: Money a company has borrowed from banks, bonds and other lenders.
- [[debt-to-equity|Debt to Equity Ratio]]: How much a company has borrowed for each rupee of the owners' own money.
- [[equity-capital|Equity Capital]]: The face value of all shares issued, shown at the top of the owners' funds.
- [[fixed-assets-and-cwip|Fixed Assets and CWIP]]: Long-life assets a company owns, and the ones it is still building.
- [[reserves|Reserves]]: Profit a company has kept in the business over the years instead of paying it out.

## Returns
- [[roce|Return on Capital Employed]]: The profit a company earns on all the money tied up in the business, owners' and lenders'.
- [[roe|Return on Equity]]: The profit a company earns each year on the owners' money.

## Cash flow
- [[cash-flow-statement|Cash Flow Statement]]: Shows the actual cash coming in and going out, split into operating, investing and financing.
- [[cash-from-financing|Cash from Financing]]: Cash raised from or paid back to lenders and owners.
- [[cash-from-investing|Cash from Investing]]: Cash spent on or received from long-term assets and investments.
- [[cash-from-operations|Cash from Operations]]: Cash the core business generated in a year.
- [[free-cash-flow|Free Cash Flow]]: Cash left after paying for the business to keep running and growing.

## Working capital
- [[cash-conversion-cycle|Cash Conversion Cycle]]: The number of days between paying for stock and collecting cash from customers.
- [[days-payable|Days Payable]]: How many days the company takes to pay its suppliers.
- [[debtor-days|Debtor Days]]: How many days of sales are still owed to the company by its customers.
- [[inventory-days|Inventory Days]]: How many days stock sits in the company before it is sold.
- [[working-capital|Working Capital]]: The short-term money tied up in running the business day to day.

## Growth and time periods
- [[cagr|CAGR]]: The steady yearly growth rate that would turn a starting number into an ending number.
- [[ttm|TTM (Trailing Twelve Months)]]: The total for the most recent four quarters, whatever the calendar year.

## Ownership
- [[dii|DII (Domestic Institutional Investor)]]: Large Indian institutions such as mutual funds and insurers that invest in shares.
- [[fii|FII (Foreign Institutional Investor)]]: Large overseas funds that invest in Indian shares.
- [[promoter-holding|Promoter Holding]]: The percentage of a company owned by the people or family who founded and control it.
- [[shareholding-pattern|Shareholding Pattern]]: A breakdown of who owns a company's shares: promoters, institutions and the public.

## General
- [[consolidated-vs-standalone|Consolidated vs Standalone]]: Whether results include the company's subsidiaries or only the parent.
- [[credit-rating|Credit Rating]]: An agency's opinion on how safe a borrower's debt is.
- [[crore-and-lakh|Crore and Lakh]]: The Indian way of writing large numbers, and how to convert them.
- [[ncd|NCD (Non-Convertible Debenture)]]: A loan note a company sells to investors that pays fixed interest and cannot be converted into shares.
