> [!definition] Net Profit
> Net profit (profit after tax, PAT) is the last line of the [[profit-and-loss|Profit and Loss Statement]]: sales minus all costs, interest, depreciation and tax.

## Explain

It is called the 'bottom line'. It belongs to the owners of the business, who may take it as dividends or leave it in the company as [[reserves|Reserves]].

On consolidated statements, net profit includes the share of subsidiaries' profit that belongs to outside owners. EPS uses only the part for the company's own shareholders (see [[eps|Earnings Per Share]]).

> [!formula] Net profit
> $$\text{Net profit} = \text{PBT} - \text{Tax}$$
> - PBT: see [[profit-before-tax|Profit Before Tax]]

> [!example] Margin and growth
> Net profit margin FY26: 95,754 ÷ 10,55,780 = 9.1%.
>
> FY26 over FY25: 95,754 ÷ 81,309 − 1 = 17.8% growth.
>
> The last four quarters (88,167) are lower than FY26 (95,754) because the strong June 2025 quarter has dropped out of the window.
