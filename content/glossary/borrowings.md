> [!definition] Borrowings
> Borrowings are the loans, bonds and debentures a company has taken, which must be repaid with interest.

## Explain

Debt lets a company grow faster than its own money alone would allow. But the interest has to be paid in good years and bad ones, so too much debt makes a business fragile.

Companies borrow from banks, by issuing bonds or [[ncd|NCD (Non-Convertible Debenture)]]s, and from overseas lenders.

## Example

> [!example] Debt over time
> Borrowings fell from 4,51,664 crore in March 2023 to 3,50,719 crore a year later, then rose again to 4,02,962.
>
> Against owners' funds of 9,04,030 crore this is a [[debt-to-equity|Debt to Equity Ratio]] ratio of about 0.45.

> [!warning] Total debt vs net debt
> Some analysts subtract cash to get 'net debt'. Screener's borrowings figure does not do this.
