> [!definition] DII (Domestic Institutional Investor)
> Domestic institutional investors (DIIs) are Indian mutual funds, insurance companies, banks and pension funds that buy shares with money collected from people.

## Explain

When you start a monthly SIP in a mutual fund, your money flows through a DII into shares. Rising SIP flows have made DIIs a powerful buyer in recent years, balancing out foreign selling.

They hold shares for savers, so their buying is steady and large.

## Data: Reliance

> [!in-practice] Reliance Industries in numbers
