> [!definition] Crore and Lakh
> A lakh is 1,00,000 (one hundred thousand). A crore is 1,00,00,000 (ten million), which is 100 lakh. A lakh crore is a trillion (10^12).

## Explain

Indian commas group digits as 3, then 2, then 2: 15,80,194 is fifteen lakh eighty thousand one hundred ninety-four. International style would write 1,580,194.

Company results on Screener are mostly in crore. Global data uses millions and billions, so conversion is needed.

## Data: Reliance

> [!in-practice] Reliance Industries in numbers
> - Reliance market cap ₹15,80,194 crore = ₹15.8 lakh crore = about ₹15.8 trillion
> - FY26 sales ₹10,55,780 crore = about ₹10.6 lakh crore

*Source: [Screener.in](https://www.screener.in/company/RELIANCE/consolidated/), Reliance Industries, consolidated, viewed 3 Oct 2026. Amounts in ₹ crore unless stated. Used to show how to read the numbers; this is not investment advice.*

## Example

> [!example] Converting
> 1 crore = 10 million. 1 lakh crore = 1 trillion.
>
> 15,80,194 crore × 10 million = 1.58 × 10^13 rupees = 15.8 trillion.

> Screener prints 'Figures in Rs. Crores' above each table. Always read it before comparing.
