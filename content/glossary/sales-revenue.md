> [!definition] Sales (Revenue)
> Sales (revenue or turnover) is the total amount customers paid the company for its products and services in a period, before any costs are deducted.

## Explain

It is called the 'top line' because it sits at the top of the [[profit-and-loss|Profit and Loss Statement]]. Everything else is subtracted from it.

Sales tells you the size of the business and whether it is growing. It says nothing on its own about profit: a company can grow sales and still lose money.

## Data: Reliance

> [!in-practice] Reliance Industries in numbers
> - FY26 sales ₹10,55,780 crore; last four quarters ₹11,23,055 crore
> - FY15 ₹3,74,372 crore, FY16 ₹2,72,583 crore, FY22 ₹6,94,673 crore
> - June 2026 quarter ₹3,09,468 crore, up 27% from ₹2,43,632 crore a year earlier

*Source: [Screener.in](https://www.screener.in/company/RELIANCE/consolidated/), Reliance Industries, consolidated, viewed 3 Oct 2026. Amounts in ₹ crore unless stated. Used to show how to read the numbers; this is not investment advice.*

## Example

> [!example] Sales can fall as well as rise
> From FY15 to FY16 Reliance's sales dropped from 3,74,372 to 2,72,583 crore, a fall of 27%. Refining makes up much of its sales and refinery sales follow oil prices, so a fall in oil prices lowers sales even if volumes hold.
>
> From FY16 to FY26 sales grew about 3.9 times (see [[cagr|CAGR]]).

> [!warning] Sales in a commodity business
> For oil and gas companies sales move with commodity prices. Judge the business on [[operating-profit-margin|Operating Profit Margin]], not sales alone.
