> [!definition] Consolidated vs Standalone
> Standalone results cover only the parent company itself. Consolidated results combine the parent with all the companies it controls (subsidiaries), as if they were one business.

## Explain

Reliance Industries owns controlling stakes in Jio Platforms, Reliance Retail and many more. Its standalone report would miss most of them. The consolidated report adds them all together, so it shows the true size of the group.

Consolidated is what you normally use to judge a group. Standalone is useful to see the parent's own numbers.

## Example

> [!example] Where the minority share appears
> When Reliance owns only 66.43% of Jio Platforms, consolidated results still include 100% of Jio's sales and profit, then show how much belongs to outside owners.
>
> That explains why consolidated net profit (₹95,754 crore) is higher than the profit EPS is based on (see [[eps|Earnings Per Share]]).

> [!warning] Match the type
> Compare consolidated with consolidated. Using a consolidated profit with a standalone share count gives nonsense ratios.
