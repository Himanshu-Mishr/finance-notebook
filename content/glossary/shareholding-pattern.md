> [!definition] Shareholding Pattern
> The shareholding pattern shows which groups hold what percentage of a company: promoters, FIIs, DIIs, government and the public. Companies must report it every quarter.

## Explain

It answers: who owns this company, and are they buying or selling? Rising institutional holdings can signal confidence. A falling promoter stake can raise questions.

Public means ordinary shareholders such as you. The total always adds up to 100%.

> [!example] Reading the chart
> The promoter band is almost flat at 50%. Over about three years FIIs shrank and DIIs grew almost by the same amount.
>
> Retail and other public holding stayed near 11%. The number of shareholders nearly doubled since 2017, which means many small holders each own a small slice.
