> [!definition] Cash from Investing
> Cash from investing (CFI) records cash used to buy plants, machines and investments, and cash received from selling them.

## Explain

For a growing company this number is usually negative. That is good news: the company is spending to expand. A positive number means it is selling assets.

Capex, the spending on fixed assets, is the biggest part for most companies.

## Example

> [!example] Growth spending
> Over FY15 to FY26 Reliance's investing cash flow was negative every single year, a total of about ₹11 lakh crore invested.
>
> Screener's free cash flow of 70,023 crore with operating cash of 1,92,113 crore implies capex of about 1,22,090 crore in FY26 (the investing total is smaller because it also nets other items, such as sales of investments).
