> [!definition] Other Income
> Other income is income from sources outside the core operations: interest on investments and deposits, dividends received, profit on selling assets, and one-off gains.

## Explain

It is listed separately so you can see how much profit comes from the main business and how much from side items.

Treat other income with care. Some of it is steady (interest on cash). Some is a one-off that will not repeat, which can make one year's profit look better than the business really is.

## Example

> [!example] One quarter, one big jump
> In the June 2025 quarter other income was 15,119 crore and net profit 30,783 crore. In the neighbouring quarters other income was around 4,500 and net profit about 22,000.
>
> Most of that jump in profit came from other income, not from selling more. If you used that quarter's profit to calculate [[pe-ratio|P/E Ratio]], the stock would look cheaper than it is.

> [!critical] Strip out one-offs
> When profit jumps, check other income first. Profit that cannot be repeated should not be used to value a company.
