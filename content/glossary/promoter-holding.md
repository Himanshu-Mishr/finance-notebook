> [!definition] Promoter Holding
> Promoters are the founders or controlling shareholders of a company. Promoter holding is the percentage of shares they and their group own.

## Explain

In India, many listed companies are family-controlled. A high promoter holding means they have a lot at stake. A falling holding can mean they are selling, or that new shares have been issued.

'Pledged' shares are shares promoters have used as security for loans. A high pledge is a warning sign.

## Example

> [!example] Controlling a conglomerate
> At about 50%, the promoter group holds half of every rupee of profit that is paid out and can decide most shareholder votes.
>
> Reliance in turn holds 66.43% of Jio Platforms, 83.56% of Reliance Retail Ventures and 59.59% of JioStar. The stakes are layered.
