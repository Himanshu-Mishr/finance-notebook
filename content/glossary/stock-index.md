> [!definition] Stock Index
> A stock index tracks the combined price of a chosen group of shares. Nifty 50 (NSE) holds 50 large companies and Sensex (BSE) holds 30.

## Explain

One share tells you about one company. An index tells you about the market. If the Nifty rises 1% on a day, large Indian companies gained about 1% in total.

Bigger companies carry more weight, so the largest names move the index most. Index funds and ETFs simply buy the shares in an index.

## Example

> [!example] Why a big stock moves the index
> Suppose a company makes up 10% of an index and its price rises 3%. That alone lifts the index about 0.3%.
