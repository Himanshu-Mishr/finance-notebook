> [!definition] Reserves
> Reserves are the accumulated profits that have not been paid out as dividends, plus premium received on shares and similar items. They belong to the owners.

## Explain

Each year, the profit not paid as dividends is added to reserves. Over decades this becomes the biggest part of the owners' money.

Reserves are not cash in a drawer. The money has been put to work in plants, shops, investments and working capital.

## Data: Reliance

> [!in-practice] Reliance Industries in numbers
> - ₹8,90,498 crore (March 2026), about 98% of owners' funds
> - ₹2,15,556 crore in March 2015

## Example

> [!example] Why reserves grow
> Reliance pays out about 10% of profit and keeps about 90%. FY26: 90% of about ₹80,770 crore is about ₹72,700 crore added to reserves.
>
> From March 2025 to 2026 reserves rose 8,29,668 → 8,90,498, an increase of about ₹60,800 crore. The gap versus ₹72,700 crore reflects other adjustments recorded directly in equity; a precise split needs the annual report.
