> [!definition] Compound Interest
> Interest calculated on the original principal **plus** all the interest already added. Simple interest, by contrast, is calculated on the original principal only.

| Year | Simple (8%) | Compound (8%) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₹1,08,000 | ₹1,08,000 |
| 2 | ₹1,16,000 | ₹1,16,640 |
| 3 | ₹1,24,000 | ₹1,25,971 |

The gap starts small and keeps widening. This is what drives [[future-value]]. More frequent compounding (monthly, daily) increases the result slightly; see [[01-time-value-of-money|Time Value of Money]].
