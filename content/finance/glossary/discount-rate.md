> [!definition] Discount Rate
> The annual rate used to bring a future amount back to today's value. It represents the return you could earn elsewhere on a similar-risk investment.

- Higher discount rate → lower [[present-value]].
- Riskier cash flows are discounted at a higher rate.

> [!note]
> How to *choose* a discount rate (risk-free rate, cost of capital) is covered later in [[finance/04-valuation/index|Valuation]].
